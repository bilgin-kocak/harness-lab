# Lean 4 proving on a pinned miniF2F subset: held-out result

> **DRAFT, under review.** The numbers are being independently traced, and the `reproduce.py` run

**Status: verified subset result. Lean-error feedback improves the verified rate.**

These are results on 40 held-out miniF2F test problems with one model. They are not a leaderboard
score.

**Where the numbers come from:**
- Every held-out statistic below is in
  [`results.json`](../../studies/2026-11-math-minif2f/results.json), which
  `harnesslab study report` computes from the records. The rendered tables are in
  [`tables.md`](../../studies/2026-11-math-minif2f/tables.md).
- The dev tallies, failure types, stops, per-call counts and times are computed from the raw
  records: `smoke/`, `dev/` and `held_out/attempts.jsonl.gz`.
- [Reproduce](#reproduce) re-derives the statistics and re-checks the proofs.

## Summary

- **System under test.** Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`, medium effort) writes a Lean 4
  tactic proof for each miniF2F theorem. The Lean 4.24.0 kernel, with Mathlib, checks it.
- **The search.** It ran on 30 dev problems (the `valid` split) and compared:
  - the baseline (one attempt, no feedback);
  - F: the baseline plus up to 3 rounds of feeding Lean's error messages back to the model;
  - F plus one more knob each: lemma retrieval, tactic probes, planning, a 48k-token context,
    or 2 retries.
- **Dev outcome.**
  - **Stage 1:** F and four of its variants proved all 30 problems; the baseline proved 26.
  - **No combination:** no single-knob addition beat F, so nothing was combined.
  - **Confirm:** with 2 more repeats each, F scored 0.989 and the baseline 0.922. F was frozen.
- **Held-out comparison.** The comparison covers 40 `test` problems, 3 repeats each, with F
  (frozen) against the baseline:

  | | Baseline | F (frozen) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Verified attempts | 102 of 120 (0.850) | 117 of 120 (0.975) |
  | Problems verified at least once (pass@3) | 36 of 40 | 39 of 40 |
  | Problems verified in at least 2 of 3 repeats | 35 of 40 | 39 of 40 |
  | Cost per verified proof | $0.064 | $0.113 |

  - **Primary test (pre-registered, plan A10).** The per-problem verified rate rises by
    **+0.125** [95% CI +0.042, +0.225]. 8 problems improve, 0 get worse and 32 tie (exact sign
    test p = 0.0078).
  - **Secondary test.** McNemar on the per-problem majority outcome: 4 problems flip to verified
    and none flip back, p = 0.125. That is not significant.
  - **Cost.** Feedback about doubles the cost: $13.20 for F against $6.53 for the baseline on
    held-out.
- **Verification.** The Lean kernel decided every outcome.
  - The theorem statement is spliced from the untouched problem file.
  - `sorry`, `admit` and new axioms are refused.
  - Only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` may appear among the axioms used.
- **Clean run.** No refusals, API errors or harness errors. The held-out phase ran once and is
  complete: 240 of 240 cells. All 704 API calls were served by `claude-opus-5-5`.

## What was compared

- **Baseline.** Config hash `980e935d0d7e`:
  - one try, no feedback rounds, no planning, no retrieval, no tactic tools;
  - a 12,000-token context limit.
- **Frozen configuration.** `F`, config hash `37f78cb9d35f`: the baseline with
  `feedback_rounds = 3`. When Lean rejects a proof, its first error messages and the failed proof
  go back to the model, up to 3 times. Everything else is equal.
- **Objective (dev only, plan A6).**
  - **Feasibility.** A configuration is feasible only if its mean cost per attempt is at most 4×
    the baseline's.
  - **Ranking.** Feasible configurations are ranked by the mean per-problem verified rate.
  - **Ties.** Within 1/30, the lower cost per verified proof wins.
  - **Eligibility.** After a confirm stage, the freeze considers only the confirmed
    configurations: F and the baseline.

## Protocol

1. **Split.** Problems are ranked by `sha256(f"{seed}:{split}:{id}")`. The first 30 of `valid`
   are dev and the first 40 of `test` are held-out.
   - **Leakage checks.** `split.json` records no shared IDs and no shared statements.
   - **Before any paid run**, `harnesslab study check` confirmed that no held-out theorem name or
     statement appears in the prompts or in the Mathlib retrieval index.
2. **Smoke test.** 3 dev problems with the baseline and with every knob on, 1 repeat, $0.35. The
   owner approved the full run after seeing its measured cost and the projected total (plan A9).
3. **Search, on dev problems only.** Each stage's plan or decision was written once and committed
   (`dev/plans/`):
   - **`stage1`:** 7 configurations × 30 problems × 1 repeat. Committed in `4752149`, 8 s after
     its first cell.
   - **`combine`:** this would have combined every single-knob addition that beat F by at least 2
     problems. None did, so it decided to run nothing (`08a1651`).
   - **`confirm`:** F (the stage-1 winner) and the baseline, 2 more repeats each (`2a1b60f`).
4. **Freeze.** `freeze.json` records:
   - the frozen configuration and the baseline;
   - the sealed spec sections and the hash of the 330 dev records;
   - the adapter's provenance (model, prices, prompts, verifier and Lean pins);
   - the split's hash.

   The held-out phase re-checks all of these before running a cell. It found no problem.
5. **Held-out, once.** F and the baseline each ran on 40 problems × 3 repeats, in a seeded
   order: 240 cells, with no harness error and no rerun.
6. **Report.** The per-problem verified rate averages the 3 repeats within a problem. The report
   computes:
   - the mean difference with a 95% percentile bootstrap over problems;
   - an exact sign test on the problems that differ;
   - McNemar on the majority outcome (at least 2 of 3 repeats).

## Verifier

The Lean 4.24.0 kernel with Mathlib `f897ebc` decides every outcome (plan A7):

- **Statement.** The model returns only the tactic block. The harness splices it after the
  untouched problem file's `:= by`, so the statement is byte-identical to the benchmark's.
- **Screening.** Before compiling, the proof is screened. It must not contain `sorry`, `admit`,
  `axiom` or other metaprogramming words, or the commands `#eval`, `#exit`, `#print` and
  `#guard_msgs`. Only a few resource `set_option`s are allowed.
- **Pass condition.** A pass needs all of:
  - an error-free compile;
  - `#print axioms` of the theorem listing only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`;
  - no timeout (300 s).
- **Red team.** A red team attacked this verifier with the real Lean 4.24.0 and Mathlib before
  any paid run, and found no false pass. Its three reports of valid proofs being rejected (a
  denied word in a comment, a tab, an unscoped `set_option`) were judged not defects: the
  screening is deliberately strict.

**What it does not check:**
- whether a miniF2F statement formalizes its informal problem correctly. The port has had
  statement fixes; the pinned commit includes the latest ones.
- anything about the model's reasoning other than the final proof.

## Results on held-out problems

Frozen (F) against the baseline on all 40 problems. Higher is better.

| Comparison | n | Rate baseline | Rate F | Diff [95% CI] | W/L/T | Sign p | Majority only F / only baseline | McNemar p |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| frozen vs baseline | 40 | 0.85 | 0.975 | +0.125 [+0.0417, +0.225] | 8/0/32 | 0.0078 | 4 / 0 | 0.125 |

| Per configuration | Baseline | F |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified attempts (pass@1) | 102/120 = 0.850 | 117/120 = 0.975 |
| pass@3 (verified in any repeat) | 36/40 | 39/40 |
| Problems with mixed outcomes across repeats | 5 | 0 |
| Median / mean wall time per attempt | 8.2 s / 22.4 s | 15.4 s / 55.2 s |
| API calls per attempt (mean) | 1.00 | 1.23 |
| Cost per attempt / per verified proof | $0.054 / $0.064 | $0.110 / $0.113 |
| Refusals | 0 | 0 |
| `max_tokens` stops | 4 | 12 |

**Reading.**
- **The primary test is significant.** Lean-error feedback raised the per-problem verified rate
  on every problem where the two differ: 8 of 8. The interval excludes 0.
- **The majority-outcome McNemar test is not significant (p = 0.125).** Four problems change
  their majority outcome, all in F's favour. With 4 discordant problems, p = 0.125 is the
  smallest value the exact test can give.
- **Cost.** Feedback roughly doubles the cost per verified proof, from $0.064 to $0.113. It adds
  1.23 calls per attempt against 1.00, and longer runs.
- **Scope.** The effect is the effect of feedback rounds for this model on these problems. It
  says nothing about the other knobs, which dev could not separate (see Negative results).

## Cost and latency

- **API cost:** $34.01 of the $150 cap (smoke $0.35, dev $13.93, held-out $19.73).
  - Costs are computed from the token usage the API reported, at the pinned prices: $4 per
    million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, with cache reads and writes at their
    listed rates, as of 2026-11-03.
  - They were not reconciled with the Anthropic invoice.
- **Time:**

  | Phase | Window (UTC) | Cell-hours |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Dev | 13:37–14:30 | 3.36 |
  | Held-out | 14:30–15:30 | 3.07 |

  Four attempts ran in parallel, with up to 3 Lean processes.
- **Per attempt on held-out:** median wall time 8.2 s for the baseline and 15.4 s for F. Each
  final Lean check takes about 5 s more.

## Failures

**Held-out, F:** 3 failures, all on imosl_2007_algebra_p6. No proof was produced: its 12
`max_tokens` stops are all on this problem.

**Held-out, baseline:** 18 failures, on 9 problems:
- 14 Lean errors. Examples: unsolved goals on amc12a_2013_p4, `maximum recursion depth` on
  amc12_2000_p1, a cast mismatch on aime_1991_p9.
- 4 with no proof produced: 3 on imosl_2007_algebra_p6 and 1 on imo_1997_p5. These are the
  baseline's 4 `max_tokens` stops.

`tables.md` lists every failed cell with Lean's message.

**Dev:** 9 failures out of 330 cells.
- In stage 1: 4 baseline Lean errors, and 1 F + 48k-context attempt that hit the 300 s Lean
  timeout.
- In confirm: 1 F and 3 baseline Lean errors.
- No refusal, no API error and no harness error in any phase.

## Negative results

- **Dev was at its ceiling.** F and four of its single-knob variants each proved all 30 dev
  problems on one repeat. Dev therefore could not tell apart retrieval, tactic probes, planning
  or 2 retries, which all cost more than F. Combine ran nothing, and none of these knobs is
  shown to help.
- **48k context.** F with a 48k-token context proved 29 of 30, one fewer, because one Lean check
  timed out.
- **Hard problems.** One held-out problem, imosl_2007_algebra_p6, was not proved by either
  configuration in any repeat.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Plan A13**, recorded before any paid call:
  - API failures that say nothing about the configuration count as `unavailable` (a harness
    error), not as failed proofs. None occurred.
  - The key and the endpoint are explicit, and the key is read from
    `HARNESSLAB_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, because cloud sessions hide `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
- **Held-out reserve.** It was set to $17.53 from the smoke test, before stage 1, as the plan
  says.
- **Not searched:** effort levels other than `medium`, and other models. The plan fixes one model
  and one effort level.
- **Operational fix during the run, no effect on any record.** After the smoke run,
  `harnesslab study search` exited with code 1 because it closed the SDK client on another event
  loop. It was fixed in `531e810`, before stage 1.

## Limitations

- **Contamination.** miniF2F is public and older than the model, so the model may have seen
  these problems or their solutions.
  - The comparison is paired (same model, same problems), so it measures the harness's feedback
    loop, not the model's knowledge.
  - Absolute rates (0.85 and 0.975) may still be inflated by memorization.
- **Small subset.**
  - 40 held-out problems, 3 repeats, one model, one effort level and one Lean version.
  - The held-out rates are near the ceiling (0.85 and 0.975), so larger gains were not possible
    to show.
- **Sampling is not seedable.** A rerun of the attempts would give different proofs, so the
  repeats measure that variability. `reproduce.py` re-checks the recorded proofs and statistics;
  it does not re-sample.
- **Costs are at pinned list prices**, not invoice amounts.
- **The search found what the plan's reference configuration already was.** F, the baseline plus
  feedback, was named in the plan, and the held-out result tests it. The other knobs could not be
  separated on dev.

## Reproduce

```bash
bash studies/2026-11-math-minif2f/setup.sh
export PATH="$HOME/.elan/bin:$PATH"
uv run python studies/2026-11-math-minif2f/reproduce.py <miniF2F-lean4 checkout at 5746b7d> --reverify all
```

The script makes no API call. It checks six things, and prints PASS or FAIL for each:
1. It re-derives `split.json` from the checkout.
2. It re-checks every freeze check, except that `freeze.json` is committed: its scratch copy is
   not a git checkout. Check that one with
   `git log -- studies/2026-11-math-minif2f/freeze.json`, which shows `267eb70`.
3. It recomputes the held-out comparison from the committed records.
4. It checks that the held-out phase is complete.
5. It checks the total spend against the cap.
6. It re-checks every recorded held-out proof with the Lean kernel.
